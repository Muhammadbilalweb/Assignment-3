What is Formula 1?

Formula 1, also known as F1, is an international motor racing championship. It uses specially designed single-seat racing cars.

Important Features

  1. High-speed racing
  2. Professional drivers
  3. Advanced car technology
  4. International racing circuits
  5. Team competition
